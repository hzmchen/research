## Executive summary

- **Yes, there is a live API** — three anonymous **OGC SensorThings (Fraunhofer
  FROST)** servers on `api.viz.berlin.de`, but you must use the *right* one,
  because Berlin is **mid-migration from infrared TEU detectors to thermal
  cameras**:
  | Server (`/v1.1/`) | What | Sites | Newest obs (2026-05-31) | State |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | **`FROST-Server-ThermiCam`** | **NEW** thermal cams, **multimodal** (PKW, LKW, Lieferwagen, Krad, **Fahrrad, Fußgänger**), 5-min | **67** | **2026-05-31** (today) | ✅ **live now** |
  | `FROST-Server-TEU` | **LEGACY** infrared detectors, KFZ/PKW/LKW, 5-min | 276 | ~**2025-07-02** | ⛔ **frozen / being retired** |
  | `FROST-Server-EcoCounter2` | bike counters | 24 | **2026-05-30** | ✅ live |
- **So my earlier "live to mid-2025" conclusion was wrong.** The date really is
  May 2026 (verified). The **TEU feed is stale because the sensors are being
  decommissioned**; current vehicle data lives on the **ThermiCam** server — but
  only at **67 sites so far** (partial rollout), vs the 276 TEU cross-sections.
- **History — a public Azure Blob container:**
  `https://mdhopendata.blob.core.windows.net/verkehrsdetektion/` (the page at
  `api.viz.berlin.de/daten/verkehrsdetektion` is a JS browser over it). Curated
  **hourly** values, batched monthly, **ends `detektor_2025_06.tgz`** — i.e. the
  archive **stopped ~mid-2025 too** and has not been extended through the migration.
- **⚠️ Torstraße has NO thermal camera yet** → its only source is the now-frozen
  TEU feed. **There is currently no live Torstraße data**; the most recent is
  **2025-07-02** (TEU SensorThings) / **June 2025** (archive). See
  [`torstrasse-live-query.md`](torstrasse-live-query.md).
- **Two quality regimes** in the archive: *alte Qualitätssicherung* (per-lane +
  aggregated cross-section CSVs) and *neue Qualitätssicherung* (per-detector CSV in
  a monthly `.tgz`, extra QA metrics; **historical data reworked in 2025**).
- **Tools ecosystem is thin.** The authoritative code is DPS's own
  (`datenablage`, `eco-counter`, `masterportal-addon-sensor-chart`). Third-party
  repos that target *this* detector feed are scarce/dormant — you'll mostly write
  your own `curl` glue (which is easy; see the worked example).
- **Licence:** dl-de/by-2.0, attribution *"Digitale Plattform Stadtverkehr Berlin /
  Verkehrsdetektion Berlin"*.
- **Torstraße has exactly 8 detectors** = 4 cross-sections (TE180, TE181, TE410,
  TE431) × 2 lanes each. We pulled June 2025 for one (`TEU00410_Det0`,
  Torstraße @ Rosa-Luxemburg-Platz) with `curl | tar` — see the showcase.

## Why is the data old / the endpoints stale?

The evidence points to a **hardware migration**, not a platform outage:

1. **The detector technology is being swapped.** SenUVK/VIZ are replacing the
   **passive-infrared TEU "Traffic Eye Universal"** units (>300 installed) with
   **thermal-imaging cameras (FLIR ThermiCam)** that additionally detect cyclists
   and pedestrians. The data confirms it: the `ThermiCam` server is multimodal and
   **live today**, while the legacy `TEU` server **froze ~2025-07-02** — i.e. the
   old detectors are being switched off as cameras take over.
2. **It's not the platform.** The **EcoCounter (bike) FROST server on the same host
   is current to 2026-05-30**, so the infrastructure is healthy — only the *vehicle
   TEU* feed stopped.
3. **The new rollout is partial.** ThermiCam covers only **67 sites** vs 276 TEU
   cross-sections, so during the transition citywide vehicle coverage has actually
   **shrunk**, and many streets (incl. **Torstraße**) currently have **no live
   detector** at all.
4. **The history pipeline lagged through the change.** The monthly blob archive was
   **"reworked in 2025"** (per its own ReadMe) and **was not extended past
   2025-06** — consistent with publishing being paused/re-tooled during migration.

**Caveat (confidence):** the timestamps, server states, site counts, and the
Torstraße gap are *verified by live calls*. The *migration* as the root cause is a
strong inference from those facts + the documented thermal-camera programme; I did
**not** find an official notice dating the TEU decommissioning or promising when
ThermiCam/the archive will reach full coverage.

## ⚠️ The duplicate-row gotcha (archive)

In the *neue Qualitätssicherung* per-detector CSVs, **hours with a valid reading
appear twice** — once as an all-`NaN` placeholder and once with the real values.
In our sample, **418 of 715** hours were duplicated like this. **Always dedupe on
`(Datum, Stunde)` and prefer the non-`NaN` row.** Details and proof in
[`torstrasse-live-query.md`](torstrasse-live-query.md).

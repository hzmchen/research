## Method & caveats

Per detector the tables give the **first** and **last** 5-minute cross-section
observation, the **active span**, the **number of datastreams**, and an inferred
**possible observation count** (see below). Timestamps come from actual
observations (`$orderby` asc/desc, `$top=1`) or — where verified reliable — the
server-maintained `phenomenonTime` interval; everything was fetched with a handful
of **bulk queries**, never per-site loops (see [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) →
*Be a good citizen*).

- **Sample frequency: confirmed 5 minutes** (consecutive obs at :00/:05/:10…). Each
  detector also exposes the same series **aggregated** to hour/day/week/month/year.
- **Fill-rate within active periods ≈ 100 %** (measured 287/288 in a sample day,
  2016/2016 in a sample week).
- **Datastreams per detector:** ThermiCam **216–648** (median 432); TEU **72–144** (median 108). These factor as
  *(vehicle classes) × (count & speed) × (6 time-aggregations) × (cross-section + lanes)* —
  ThermiCam has 9 classes (incl. Fahrrad/Fußgänger), TEU has 3 (KFZ/PKW/LKW).

### "Possible observations" — what the number means
A **theoretical upper bound**: every datastream sampled continuously at its nominal
cadence across `[first … last]`. Per detector:

```
possible ≈ (datastreams / 6) × span_minutes × Σ(1/cadence)
           Σ(1/cadence) over 5min,1h,1d,1wk,1mo,1yr = 0.2175 obs/min/series
```

This is an **upper bound, not the real count**: detectors have interior **gaps**
(e.g. many TEU sites have dense data into 2024 but only a sparse straggler at the
2025-07-02 cut-over), and rare classes (Bus/Fußgänger) are mostly zero. Exact counts
(`Observations?$count=true`) are too expensive to fetch server-wide without
overloading the API — hence the inference. Treat these as *"how much the schema
could hold"*, with the real volume some fraction lower.
